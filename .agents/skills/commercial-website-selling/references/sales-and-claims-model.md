# Sales and claims model

Use this reference when a page connects websites to acquisition, qualified contacts, advertising, analytics, conversion, attribution, or economic return.

## Website responsibilities in the selling process

A visitor may enter from search, advertising, referral, social content, or an existing relationship. The website may support traffic qualification, preparation before contact, first-screen continuity, reciprocal qualification, presentation, objection handling, the next commitment, follow-up, proposal, onboarding, and service.

Not every page must perform every job. Judge it by its role, traffic source, and visitor decision stage.

## Evidence hierarchy

Prefer:

1. campaign, query, referral, and landing-page data;
2. analytics segmented by source, page, device, and meaningful event;
3. enquiries, calls, proposals, CRM stages, revenue, and lost-sale reasons;
4. interviews and recorded objections;
5. observed page and path content;
6. general heuristics.

Do not infer causal commercial performance from static copy or design.

## Claim ladder

### Level 1: verified capability

Examples include configuring events for forms, telephone links, email links, and booking actions; building a page for a defined offer; connecting an authorised analytics or CRM system; and defining page structure, copy, design, development, and launch in one engagement.

State this level only when repository, service scope, or supplied evidence confirms it.

### Level 2: observable output

Examples include seeing which tracked source or page preceded a recorded request, making audience and offer explicit, and having one directly responsible professional handle the documented work.

State the conditions: consent, configuration, channel coverage, access, and available data.

### Level 3: plausible business contribution

Examples include reducing uncertainty before contact, helping attract or qualify more relevant requests, reducing avoidable supplier coordination, and making acquisition performance easier to evaluate.

Use language such as `progettato per`, `può aiutare`, or `rende possibile verificare`. Do not present the contribution as an achieved result.

### Level 4: measured outcome

Examples include an increase in qualified enquiries, lower acquisition cost, higher conversion rate, or attributable revenue and ROI.

State only with verified measurement, period, baseline, sample, attribution method, and relevant caveats.

### Level 5: guarantee

Examples include guaranteed contacts, revenue, rankings, ROI, complete attribution down to every euro, zero waste, or zero dispersion.

Do not state unless the user supplies a real, bounded guarantee and accepts the underlying commercial obligation.

## Attribution limits

Tracking interactions is not the same as proving revenue attribution. A robust economic claim may require ad costs, consent-aware event collection, source persistence, call tracking, form and booking events, CRM linkage, sales outcomes, margins, offline interactions, repeat visits, and an agreed attribution model.

Prefer precise statements such as: `Configuro gli eventi necessari per distinguere telefonate, moduli e richieste provenienti dalle pagine monitorate.` Avoid: `Traccio ogni contatto e ogni euro investito.`

## Demonstration without case studies

The page itself can demonstrate clarity, speed, working interactions, and visible measurement features. It cannot demonstrate that it generates qualified leads or ROI merely because the visitor arrived, read, or scrolled.

When client proof is unavailable, use demonstrable evidence and label prototypes, sample outputs, or internal tools honestly.

## Proprietary framework maturity

Treat `proprietary` and `operational` as separate claims. An owned framework can exist while still being developed.

Use these states:

- `concept`: the architecture or method is defined but not yet implemented;
- `in_development`: working parts exist, while scope and behaviour are still evolving;
- `operational`: the stated capabilities are implemented and usable in real work;
- `validated`: the capability has evidence from actual use, measurement, or repeated delivery.

For an in-development framework, separate:

```yaml
current_capabilities:
planned_capabilities:
known_limits:
evidence_or_demo:
```

Acceptable wording includes `framework proprietario in sviluppo`, `moduli già utilizzabili`, and `funzioni di misurazione previste o in sperimentazione`, when true. Do not collapse planned modules into present-tense capabilities.

The framework can support a differentiated mechanism. It does not by itself prove acquisition performance, qualified leads, attribution accuracy, ROI, lower costs, or better rankings.

## Target-specific hypotheses

For local SMEs and structured professional firms, investigate rather than assume an old brochure site, weak visibility into enquiries, dissatisfaction with recurring fees or fragmented suppliers, a need for one accountable contact, or sensitivity to time, ownership, data access, support, and measurable commercial value.

Turn only confirmed hypotheses into page claims.
