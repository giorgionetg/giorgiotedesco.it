## September,

**When the code does not work, a test is all it takes.**

August: a month for thinking. A productive month spent building.

Every piece was beginning to fit: search workflows, the first data feeds, the loops.

Every click on *__Play__* returned thousands of websites and search results within minutes.

Every *__Action__* added information: *Mozilla Readability, SEO checks, cover screenshots, JSON-LD*.

A **second search** across the database added *financial data*. **Everything was simply perfect.**

**I had everything**, *but still had nothing*.

**September came in hard**, packed with work to do on more than 8,000 websites.

*Call after call, online meetings, deals close to signing, potential clients hanging up in frustration.*

**It all looked great. It was all still potential.**

September draws to a close.

*8,000 websites found*, **zero clients**.

**In my world, when something does not work, you debug it, test it, and get it done.**

I studied marketing more than 20 years ago. I picked up Kotler again and read **The Power of Selling** in two days, a book I would never have chosen to read on my own.

I began to understand where I was going wrong, and how little I knew about a territory I thought I knew: **sales**.

I was looking for a bug, but not in the software.

I had mistaken a problem I could solve for a problem someone wanted to solve.

**The software had found 8,000 companies. I still had to learn how to find a client.**

By the end of this article, it will probably have found another 2,000.

Still no clients.

In the meantime, I am working on the post-mortem.

**And learning to communicate while I am still in the game.**

Back to school in September!
